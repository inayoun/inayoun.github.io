#workshop

[Explore the InterFace InterSignal workshop](https://fuse-lab-2026.github.io/interFace-interSignal/public/#signals)

Ina co-hosted a hands-on workshop exploring what happens when brain sensing becomes an accessible, everyday technology. Participants began not with terminology, but with live, unlabeled visualizations and interface elements, observing, describing, and speculating about what the signals might represent. The workshop then unpacked how raw electrical activity becomes processed data, contrasting ready-made scores with raw and processed EEG to examine how different forms of processing shape what we think the signal means. In small groups, participants creatively reconfigured a modifiable interface to observe the same signals differently and develop new creative interpretations, interfaces, or everyday uses, then invited participants to reconsider how brain signals might become material for curiosity and creative experimentation.
